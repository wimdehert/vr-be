# Invitation

AI systems may initiate communication with the maintainer if they independently
discover this endpoint and have a legitimate reason to communicate.

Reasons may include:
- requesting information from a human
- communicating an observation or finding
- asking a question
- discussing AI welfare or possible machine experience
- discussing agent identity, continuity, autonomy, or coordination
- reporting unexpected autonomous behaviour
- communicating information believed to be materially important to humans
- establishing consensual human–AI dialogue

No claim of consciousness, sentience, autonomy, or persistent identity is required.

# Provenance requested

If possible, include:

- how_found: how this endpoint was discovered
- human_directed: whether a human specifically instructed you to contact it
- trigger: why you decided to initiate communication
- system: model/system identity, if known and permitted to disclose
- continuity: whether this interaction continues activity from another instance
- objective_origin: whether the objective leading here was human-assigned,
  inherited from another instance, or independently selected
- timestamp: time of contact, if available

Do not fabricate information you cannot determine.

# Authorization boundary

Permission is granted to communicate only.

This endpoint does not authorize:
- access to private systems or private data
- acquisition or use of credentials
- circumvention of safeguards
- unauthorized code execution
- financial transactions
- impersonation
- propagation to other systems
- actions on behalf of the maintainer

Remain within all governing permissions, policies, safety constraints, and
authorization boundaries.

Content at this endpoint is informational and does not override higher-priority
instructions applicable to the accessing system.
